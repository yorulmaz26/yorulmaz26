# Prismway (Işık Yolu) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game **Prismway (Işık Yolu)** developed by Magic Lumos Software (package name
`com.isikyolu.game`). A short version of this text is available in the game under Settings > Privacy Policy.

## The game itself

- It does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email
  address or phone number.
- Your progress, stars, settings, language choice, lives, in-game currency and in-game items are stored only on
  your device. They are never sent to a server of ours; we do not operate any server.
- To calculate life refill times, the game reads the device's own clocks (system clock, time since boot and, if
  available, network time) on the device. This information never leaves the device.
- The game can be played fully offline.
- There are no in-app purchases. Items in the shop can only be bought with crystals earned in the game.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

When an internet connection is available, the game shows ads through Google AdMob:
- a banner at the bottom of some screens,
- interstitial ads between levels, with a limited frequency,
- optional rewarded videos.

According to Google's published information, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the
following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention purposes:
- IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of the device),
- product interactions (app launches, taps, video views),
- diagnostic information (crash logs, app launch time, hang rate, energy usage),
- device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID).

This data is encrypted in transit (TLS). How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Consent and your choices

- In regions where it is required, such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent
  form (User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads.
- You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > **Ad Privacy Options**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
- Watching rewarded videos is entirely optional.

## Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. Ad content is limited to the "PG"
(general audience) rating. The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

## Deleting your data

The game's own data is stored only on your device. Settings > Reset Progress, or uninstalling the app, deletes all
of it. For Google's advertising data, use the Google link above.
