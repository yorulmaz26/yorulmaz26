## Uzun açıklama
Tap to fire your thrusters, thread the gap and see how far you can fly! Planet Gap – Gezegen Kaçışı is a one-tap space arcade game where you guide your rocket through the gaps between towering stacks of planets. Simple to learn, hard to master.

How to Play
• Tap anywhere to give your rocket a burst of thrust.
• Let go and gravity takes over – time your taps to stay on course.
• Slip through the gaps between the planet towers and rack up points.
• Reach the level's goal score to clear it. One crash and the run is over!

Features
• 500 levels with a goal score that starts at 15 and climbs gradually
• New twists in every level: fast pace, narrow gaps, dense towers, heavy or light gravity, gold rush, shifting wind, fireballs, meteor showers, swaying towers, black-hole pull and lightning
• A big map finale every 5th level
• Every level is tested to be beatable
• 10 space worlds, each with its own look, hazards and music
• Endless mode with Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty
• Local multiplayer for 2–4 players – no internet needed
• 10 crafts to unlock with coins you earn in the game
• Original synthesized music and sound effects
• Smooth, colorful vector-style space graphics
• Portrait mode and one-handed controls
• Small download, runs on Android 8.0 and later
• No account or sign-up needed

10 Space Worlds
• Earth Orbit – where your journey begins
• Ice Belt – super-low gravity for floaty flying
• Lava Sea – watch out for fireballs from below
• Gas Giants – shifting wind and giant ringed planets
• Asteroid Belt – towers of swaying asteroids
• Black Hole – a pull that drags you toward the center
• Electric Storm – dodge the lightning
• Meteor Shower – dodge a storm of incoming meteors
• Toxic Nebula – wind and swaying towers combined
• Galactic Core – the final challenge

Game Modes
• Level Mode: battle through 500 levels across all 10 worlds and hit the goal score to move on.
• Endless Mode: pick any world, choose Easy, Normal or Hard and chase your high score. Your best is saved for every map and difficulty.
• Local Multiplayer: 2–4 players, each on their own phone, connected to the same Wi-Fi or to one phone's hotspot. No internet needed. Play in split-screen view to see everyone's run side by side, or in full-screen view with your run up big and the others' scores in the corner. Crashed players keep watching – highest score wins!

Crafts
Collect coins while you fly and earn more by clearing levels. Spend them in the shop on 10 crafts: Classic Rocket, Night Rocket, Emerald Rocket, Space Shuttle, Comet, UFO, Golden Rocket, Ringed Planet, Lava Planet and Crystal Ship. Crafts are bought with coins only – never with real money.

Lives & Ads – Straight Talk
• Lives are used in Level Mode only. You have 4, and 1 life refills every 20 minutes – even while the game is closed.
• Starting a level costs a life. Clear it and you get that life back.
• Need an extra life? Watch an optional video or spend 60 coins.
• No in-app purchases: there's nothing to buy with real money.
• The game contains ads: a banner on menu screens (never during gameplay), an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional rewarded videos for +1 life or double coins.
• Single-player works offline. Ads need an internet connection.

Languages
English, Turkish, German, Italian, French and Spanish. The game picks your phone's language automatically, and you can switch it anytime in Settings.
