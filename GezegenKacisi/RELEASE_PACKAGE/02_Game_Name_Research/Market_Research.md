## Planet Escape — çakışma: medium
I found no exact Play or App Store game and no exact US mark in the indexed data, but the phrase is very generic. Dozens of hobby games use it, a 2019 Play game is called 'Planet Escape Plan', and a Polish travel agency uses 'Planet Escape' as its brand and app name (a travel class, not games). The biggest legal risk is that it sounds almost like PLANESCAPE, an established game and D&D trademark for computer games. Weak to own or defend. Its advantage is that it translates the Turkish title 'Gezegen Kaçışı' exactly.
