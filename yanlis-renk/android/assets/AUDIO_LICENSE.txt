Yanlis Renk - Audio License
===========================

All sound effects (sfx/*.ogg) and music loops (music/*.ogg) in this directory
were procedurally synthesized specifically for this project by its generator
script, tools/audio/gen_audio.py (Python 3 + numpy, encoded with ffmpeg/Vorbis).

No third-party samples, recordings, loops or sound libraries were used; every
waveform is computed from mathematical oscillators, envelopes, filtered noise
and algorithmic reverb inside that script. The audio is original work and may
be used, modified and redistributed under the project's own license.

To regenerate:  python3 -I tools/audio/gen_audio.py [TEMP_WAV_DIR]
