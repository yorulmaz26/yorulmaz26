Tap. Change color. Pass the gate. Sounds easy? Think again!

Miscolor: Yanlış Renk is a cute, casual color runner for all ages. Your little hero runs forward on its own. You only do one thing: tap anywhere on the screen to change its color. Red, Blue, Green, Yellow… and around again.

Every gate has a rule:
• "=" gate: you need the SAME color
• "≠" gate: you need a DIFFERENT color

Simple rules, quick decisions and plenty of "oops!" moments.

500 LEVELS IN 20 WORLDS
Explore 500 levels in 100 areas across 20 themed worlds: a sunny garden, candy land, cloud kingdom, ice valley, crystal cave, desert, toy town, underwater world, rainbow islands, space station, clock tower, magic library, robot city, color carnival and more. Every area ends with a big final level.

NEW TWISTS, ONE STEP AT A TIME
New mechanics arrive gradually, so you learn first and master later:
• Double and triple gates
• Speed zones
• Memory gates that hide their color as you get close
• Mirror zones that reverse the color order
• Rotating, sliding and timed gates
• Rainbow gates, hidden gates and flickering gates
• Misleading word gates and decoy gates
• Color-change pads, locked colors and forbidden colors
• Next, previous and echo color gates
• Fog, memory chain signs and colored coins
