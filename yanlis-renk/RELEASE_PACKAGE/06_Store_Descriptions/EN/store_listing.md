## 6. How to play

1. Your character runs forward automatically.
2. Tap anywhere on the screen to change its color: Red → Blue → Green → Yellow → Red…
3. Look at the next gate. "=" means you need the SAME color as the gate. "≠" means you need a DIFFERENT color.
4. Pass every gate to reach the finish. A wrong color fails the level and costs one life.
5. Watch out for new twists: memory gates hide their color, mirror zones reverse the order, word gates try to trick you.
