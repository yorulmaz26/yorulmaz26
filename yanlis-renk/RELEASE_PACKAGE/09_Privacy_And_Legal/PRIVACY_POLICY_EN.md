## 1. Summary
Miscolor does not require an account, does not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone, and has no chat. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

## 2. Data stored on your device
- Game progress, settings (language, sound, accessibility), in-game currencies, lives timer and a random installation identifier are saved in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to the developer.
- Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information.

## 3. Time verification
To protect the lives timer against clock changes, the app may contact `www.google.com` or `www.cloudflare.com` and read only the current time from the standard HTTP `Date` header. No personal data or game data is sent in these requests (normal network metadata such as your IP address is visible to these services, as with any internet connection).

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google LLC: banner ads on menus, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch.
Google's SDK may automatically collect and share data such as IP address, product interactions (e.g. ad taps), diagnostic information and device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID) for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit. See Google's disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required):** the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy options**.
- **Ad personalisation:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).
- Ads are requested with the "G" (general audiences) maximum content rating.
