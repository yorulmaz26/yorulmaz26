## Uzun açıklama (3167/4000)

Every move you make comes back to chase you.

In Doppelglow, a glowing neon hero follows your finger across the screen, and a few seconds later your shadow replays your exact path. If it touches you, the run is over. So every swipe counts twice: once to dodge what's in front of you, and once more when your past catches up.

Slip through laser gates, weave around mines and meteors, scoop up stars and diamonds, and cut it close for near-miss bonus points. Chain your moves together to push your combo multiplier all the way to x5.

How to play
• Drag anywhere on the screen to move your character with one finger
• Your shadow retraces your route a few seconds behind you
• Keep your shadow from touching you and avoid every obstacle
• Collect stars and diamonds and earn bonus points for near misses
• Build combos for a multiplier of up to x5

Endless Mode
Survive as long as you can while the speed keeps rising. Your best score and a top-10 list are saved right on your device.

Map Mode
500 levels across 100 maps. Twenty base maps return in five tiers: Classic, Night, Storm, Nightmare and Legend. Every level has two stages: collect 20 stars with one shadow on your tail, then grab 20 more while two shadows hunt you down. Each level hides 3 crowns: finish it, finish without getting hit, and reach the combo goal. That's 1,500 crowns to collect.

Maps that change the rules
• Wind that pushes you off course
• Slippery ice with real inertia
• Darkness where only a small glow lights your way
• Meteor showers, narrow passages, fast flow and extra-fast shadows

9 obstacle types
Laser walls with gaps, pulsing mines, sweeping blocks, rotating lasers, blinking laser gates, bouncing balls, meteors with warnings, orbiting twin balls and sliding gap walls.

Power-ups
• Shield absorbs one hit
• Slow Time slows everything down for a few seconds
• Magnet pulls in nearby stars and diamonds

Progress and rewards
• 12 neon characters in geometric shapes, unlocked with diamonds, and your shadow takes on your character's shape
• Player levels and XP with diamond rewards
• 7-day login streak rewards
• 3 daily missions every day, drawn from 8 mission types
• 21 achievements to unlock

Fair by design
• Diamonds are earned only by playing. There are no in-app purchases.
• Map Mode gives you up to 4 lives. One life refills every 20 minutes, even offline. Refill them all with 60 diamonds, or watch an optional video for +1 life (up to 3 per day).
• Banner ads appear only on menu screens, never during gameplay. Occasional full-screen ads show up only between runs, never in your first 3 games.
• Optional rewarded videos can double your diamonds after a run.

Also included
• Play offline (ads need an internet connection)
• 6 languages: English, Turkish, German, Italian, French and Spanish
• Interactive tutorial to learn the shadow mechanic
• Procedurally generated synthwave music, punchy sound effects and vibration, all adjustable in Settings
• No account, no chat and no location permission needed
• Clean geometric neon visuals with no violence

Stay sharp, think ahead, and don't let your shadow catch you.
