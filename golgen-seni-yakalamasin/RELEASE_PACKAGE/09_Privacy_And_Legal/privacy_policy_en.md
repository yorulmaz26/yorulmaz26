## 1. Summary

Gölgen Seni Yakalamasın is a one-finger arcade game that works offline. The game has **no accounts, no login, no chat, no user-generated content, no location permission and no in-app purchases.** The game's own code does not collect, send to our servers, or sell any personal data. The game is free and ad-supported; ads are served by Google's advertising services (Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob), which may process the data described below.

## 2. Data we do not collect

Magic Lumos Software does **not** collect through the game:

- Name, email address, phone number or any account information
- Precise (GPS) location — the game does not request a location permission
- Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
- Messages, chat content or user content
- Payment or financial information

The game's own code sends no data over the internet. Network access is used only by the ads SDK.

## 3. Data stored locally on your device

Your game progress (e.g. high score, settings, sound/vibration preferences, daily rewarded-ad counter) is stored **only on your device** using Android SharedPreferences. It is never sent to us.

The app has Android Auto Backup enabled (`allowBackup=true`). Therefore, **depending on your device's backup settings**, this local game data may be backed up to your Google account by Android's own backup service. That backup is performed by Google according to your device settings; Magic Lumos Software has no access to it. You can turn backup off in Android Settings (Settings > System/Google > Backup).

## 4. Advertising and the Google Mobile Ads SDK

The game uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to show ads:

- **Banner ads:** on menu screens only,
- **Interstitial ads:** only between runs, with frequency limits,
- **Rewarded video ads:** entirely optional; shown only if you choose to watch one (e.g. for +1 life).

No ads are shown during gameplay.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK **may collect and transmit to Google** the following data for the purposes of serving ads, measuring ad performance, analytics, and preventing fraud and abuse:

- **Device or other identifiers:** the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID and similar identifiers
- **IP address:** which may be used to estimate approximate location (country/city level)
- **App interactions:** e.g. ad impressions, ad clicks, app launches
- **Diagnostic and performance data:** crash logs, SDK performance and error information
- Technical information such as device model, OS version, language and network type

For details on how Google uses this data, see:

- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

Related permissions requested by the app: `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (to load ads), `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` (advertising ID), `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION`, `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS` (Android Privacy Sandbox ad APIs), `WAKE_LOCK` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` (added by the WorkManager library inside the ads SDK for background tasks). The game itself also uses the `VIBRATE` permission for haptic feedback.

## 5. Consent management — Google UMP

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows a consent form via the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)**. Through this form you can consent to, refuse, or manage personalized advertising and related data processing. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads in accordance with applicable rules. You can change your choice later via the in-game privacy options (where available) or by clearing the app's data.

## 6. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID

You can control your advertising ID on your Android device:

- **Android 12 and later:** Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) > "Delete advertising ID". Once deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an identifier.
- **Older versions:** Settings > Google > Ads > "Reset advertising ID" and/or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

Menu names may differ by device manufacturer. You can also manage ad personalization at <https://myadcenter.google.com>.

## 7. Children's privacy

The game is **not directed to children under 13**; the recommended audience is 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and shared data via the ads SDK, please contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 8. Data retention

- **Local game data:** remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data (any Android backup is subject to Google's backup rules).
- **Ads SDK data:** retained by Google under Google's own retention policies. Magic Lumos Software does not store this data.

## 9. Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you on any server, you can delete your local data by:

- Settings > Apps > Gölgen Seni Yakalamasın > Storage > "Clear data", or
- Uninstalling the app.

For advertising data processed by Google, you can delete/reset your advertising ID (see Section 6) and use Google's privacy tools.

## 10. Security

The game itself does not transmit data. According to Google, data sent by the ads SDK to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Local data is stored inside Android's app sandbox. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures.

## 11. Your rights — GDPR and KVKK

Under the **EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and, for users in Türkiye, the **Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK)**, you have the right to:

- Learn whether your personal data is processed and request information,
- Access your data and request its rectification or erasure,
- Request restriction of processing and object to processing,
- Withdraw consent at any time (via the UMP form or device settings),
- Data portability (GDPR),
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority; in the EEA/UK, your local data protection authority).

In the relevant regions, the legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent obtained through UMP. For requests concerning data collected by the ads SDK you can also use Google's privacy tools. Requests to Magic Lumos Software: [CONTACT EMAIL].
