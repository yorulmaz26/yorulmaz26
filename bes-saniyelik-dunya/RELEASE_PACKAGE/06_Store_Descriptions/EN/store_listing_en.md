## Full description
```
Stopwatch Shuffle is a colorful one-tap runner and platformer. Your character runs on its own and you tap to jump. But watch out: the rules of the world change every 5 seconds!

One moment gravity flips and you run on the ceiling. Then time slows down, the ground turns to ice, purple platforms blink in and out, or your character shrinks. The next rule flashes at the top of the screen in advance: get ready and jump at the right moment.

FEATURES
• 10 maps, 100 areas, 500 levels
• 10 different world rules and their combinations
• Endless World mode: how far can you go?
• 3 stars in every level and hidden collection pieces
• 20 characters, 9 trails and 8 power-ups
• Daily and weekly missions, 50 achievements, daily login rewards
• Difficulty rises steadily from easy to hard; new rules are taught one by one
• Checkpoints: a mistake doesn't send you back to the start
• Play in English, Turkish, German, Italian, French or Spanish

LIVES
A failed attempt costs 1 life; beat the level and you get it back. Lives refill every 20 minutes, even while the game is closed. The first 10 levels and Endless World don't cost lives.

PLAY OFFLINE
The whole game can be played offline. There are no real-money purchases. Rewarded ads only open when you choose; ads between levels are rare, and no ad sits on screen while you play.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap: jump. Hold: jump higher. Dodge spikes and saws, collect the stars and reach the door!

Magic Lumos Software
```

## Gameplay description
The character runs to the right on its own. The player taps to jump and holds to jump higher. Every 5 seconds a world rule changes (reverse gravity, fast world, ice, ghost platforms, bouncy world, tiny world, reverse control, slow time, shifting platforms). The goal is to reach the door without touching spikes or saws while collecting stars and coins. Each attempt has 3 hearts and checkpoints.

## Target audience
Suitable for all ages, colorful and non-violent: especially children aged 8+, families and casual players who enjoy short one-tap reflex games. (The target age group in Play Console is the developer's decision and must match the `audience` setting in `ads.properties`.)
